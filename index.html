Process

Six stages, from tree to terminal.

Sun-drying brings every batch down to export-safe moisture before sorting begins.
01

Harvest

Ripe pods are collected from partner farms within days of cutting, before quality begins to drop.

02

Sun-drying

Pods are laid out and turned daily until moisture falls to export-safe levels.

03

Hand sorting

Every batch is sorted by size and condition, and defective pods are removed.

04

Grading

Sorted pods are assigned a grade and logged against the specification sheet.

05

Bagging

Graded pods are packed into jute sacks and staged for container loading.

06

Export

Documentation is prepared and the container is booked and shipped to port.
